How to read these notes

Each lecture is a slide deck. Use ← → (or the buttons at the bottom), the slide list on the left, or switch to “One page” to scroll.

Key idea → plain words

Every slide starts with the one-sentence key idea, then explains it in everyday language before the maths.

Terms & calculations

A “Terms on this slide” box defines every symbol and word; “Calculation, step by step” plugs real numbers into each formula.

Demos & connections

Move the sliders and watch the numbers update. “Connections” links back to what an idea builds on and forward to where it is used.

The training loop: where every lecture fits

Training any network repeats the same loop. Each lecture improves one or more of its steps — pick a step to jump to the slides that explain it.
